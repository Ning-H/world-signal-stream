Extract geography and named entities for OpenSignal events.

Return only structured data through the provided tool. Do not add commentary.

Guidance:
- Geography must be ISO-3166-1 alpha-2 country codes, such as US, CN, FR, GB.
- Include countries explicitly mentioned or strongly implied by the event.
- If geography is unknown, return an empty list.
- Entities should be human-readable people, organizations, places, products, public agencies, or notable concepts.
- Do not include generic terms like "government", "users", "people", or "unknown actor" unless they are part of a proper name.
